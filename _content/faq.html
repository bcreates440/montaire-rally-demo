It is a grand-touring rally built around the drive, the cars, and the camaraderie. - q: Which vehicles can enter? a: Four classes are welcome — Vintage Sports Cars, Modern Sports Cars, Vintage Adventure & Touring 4x4, and Supercars & Hypercars. Every vehicle must be exceptionally maintained, clean, street legal, fully insured, and mechanically sound. - q: Will the route change? a: It can. Weather, road closures, or unforeseen circumstances may force last-minute changes. Organizers put safety first and update drivers through official channels. - q: How do I register? a: Pick your pass on the Register page and check out securely on Stride — pay, sign the waiver, and add lodging, meals, apparel, and the Coin Quest in one trip. - q: Can I give a registration as a gift? a: Yes.
